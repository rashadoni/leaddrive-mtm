## Design Context

### Users

LeadDrive MTM serves two operational roles. Field Agents plan and execute visits, routes, tasks, promotions, GPS-confirmed workdays, and maintain organization/contact context—often moving between clinics and pharmacies, in daylight, with intermittent connectivity, using mostly tablets and sometimes smartphones. Managers/Supervisors plan team activity, monitor coverage and GPS, review exceptions and approvals, and analyze plan-versus-fact. The product is tablet-first, fully functional on phones, localized for RU/AZ/EN, and must make data freshness, sync state, and role permissions explicit.

### Brand Personality

**Decisive · Vivid · Dependable**

The interface should make users feel oriented, capable, and in control: energetic enough for a presentation, trustworthy enough for daily field operations, and never playful at the expense of clarity.

### Aesthetic Direction

A light-first, field-ready operational aesthetic inspired by SwissMed’s functional breadth, but not its dated density. Use a strong evergreen foundation with restrained cobalt, amber, coral, and violet accents; softly tinted neutral surfaces; crisp typography; clear status color semantics; and purposeful state transitions. Tablet layouts should use rails, master/detail or list/map splits, and dense but readable operational views. Phones should adapt workflows into focused cards, bottom sheets, and full-screen detail without removing critical capability. Configurable home widgets are a signature interaction: one widget fills the workspace, two split it equally, and larger selections form a balanced responsive grid. Avoid decorative dashboards, simulated “live” data, generic card walls, and all LeadShelf/planogram functionality.

### Design Principles

1. **Role and next action first.** Agents see what to do next; Managers see team state, exceptions, and approvals. Controls and data remain server-authoritative and honestly unavailable when not connected.
2. **Adapt the workflow, not just its size.** Use measured content width, tablet master/detail patterns, phone-focused flows, and minimum 48 dp touch targets; never stretch a phone layout across a tablet.
3. **Make field truth visible.** Clearly communicate workday, GPS, offline, sync, conflict, stale-data, and last-updated states. Never present cached, preview, or incomplete information as live.
4. **Reveal operational depth progressively.** Preserve SwissMed-level filters, planning, drill-downs, and audit context through saved views, expandable filters, and contextual actions instead of overwhelming form walls.
5. **Personalize without chaos.** Keep 1–6 widgets configurable per tenant, user, role, and device class, while preserving consistent hierarchy, semantic color, accessible labels, predictable motion, and a reliable reset.
